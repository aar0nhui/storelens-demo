Data quality & privacy

Metric definitions

Visitors
Anonymous customer visits tracked in the period (staff excluded).
Impression
A visitor came inside a display's exposure zone (an opportunity to see).
Engaged
An impression where the visitor stopped in the zone long enough (default 2 s). In stop_facing mode they must also face the display.
Visitors who engaged
Share of all visitors who engaged with at least one display.
Median engaged time
Typical time an engaged visitor spent stopped at a display.
Reach
Impressions ÷ visitors. How many people come near the display.
Engagement rate
Engaged ÷ impressions. How compelling the display is once people are near it.
Engaged / 100
Engaged ÷ visitors × 100. Traffic-normalised, so a busy day doesn't flatter a display.
