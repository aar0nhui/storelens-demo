        Cameras & coverage

          Metric definitions
          Visitors
          Anonymous customer visits in the period (staff excluded).
          Impression
          A visitor came inside a display's exposure zone: an opportunity to see it.
          Engaged
          An impression where the visitor stopped in the zone for at least 2 s (in stop_facing mode, also facing it).
          Reach
          Impressions ÷ visitors: how many people come near a display.
          Stop rate
          Engaged ÷ impressions: how compelling a display is once people are near it.
          Engaged / 100
          Engaged ÷ visitors × 100. Traffic-normalised, so a busy day doesn't flatter a display. Used for A/B tests.
          Lift
          Change in engaged / 100 between two periods, adjusted for store-wide shifts, with a confidence interval.
          Age band / perceived gender
          Estimates from appearance, counted in aggregate only. Groups under the minimum size are merged or hidden.
          Index
          A group's share of a display's engaged people ÷ its share of all visitors. 1.0× = in line.
          Scenario
          "If this display matched your median display". A benchmark from your own store, not a forecast.
