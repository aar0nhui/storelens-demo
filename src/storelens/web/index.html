          Metric definitions

          Visitors
          Anonymous customer visits tracked in the period (staff excluded).
          Impression
          A visitor came inside a display's exposure zone (an opportunity to see).
          Engaged
          An impression where the visitor stopped in the zone long enough (default 2 s). In stop_facing mode they must also face the display.
          Visitors who engaged
          Share of all visitors who engaged with at least one display.
          Median engaged time
          Typical time an engaged visitor spent stopped at a display.
          Reach
          Impressions ÷ visitors. How many people come near the display.
          Engagement rate
          Engaged ÷ impressions. How compelling the display is once people are near it.
          Engaged / 100
          Engaged ÷ visitors × 100. Traffic-normalised, so a busy day doesn't flatter a display.
          Age band / perceived gender
          Estimates from appearance, counted in aggregate only. Perceived gender is what a model infers, not a fact about anyone. Groups under the minimum size are merged or hidden.
          Index
          A group's share of the people engaging with a display ÷ its share of all visitors. 1.0 = in line; 1.5 = over-represented by half.
          Scenario
          "If this display reached as many visitors (or held as many) as your median display". A benchmark from your own store, not a forecast.
          Estimated sales
          Extra engaged shoppers × the share who buy × basket size. The last two are your assumptions.
